Your Day, Traded Live

Get it on the Connect IQ Store · 2.99 €

Settings on the watch

Hold UP, pick Watch Face, select Ticker, press START, then Customize. The menu shows How it works, the three tape items, the goal line, the hours and the always-on brightness. The same settings are in Garmin Connect.

Your steps are the price

A trading screen on your wrist. Your steps of the day are the price, with their change against yesterday at the same time of day: up in green, down in red. The face notes your steps itself every quarter of an hour, so the comparison starts on the second day; until then it shows -- rather than a made-up number.

One candle per hour

A candlestick chart of your day from 06:00: one candle per hour, green when that hour had more steps than the one before, red when it had fewer. Your step goal is the dotted line. The market is open from 06:00 to 22:00 and closed the rest of the time.

The tape

Three data scroll across the tape, each your choice: watch battery, heart rate, calories, steps, Body Battery, floors climbed, distance, intensity minutes, notifications, temperature, the next sunrise or sunset, or altitude, each with an arrow showing whether it went up or down since the hour began. If your watch has no value, the tape shows --.

Always-on

A separate always-on design on a black screen: the time and your steps in grey. It shifts slightly every minute to protect the screen.

Just for fun: no financial data and no financial advice.

The face asks for the location permission for sunrise and sunset; the position never leaves the watch. Compatible with the Garmin AMOLED watches listed below. English only. Questions or a watch you would like to see supported: use the support e-mail on this page.
