KINTSUGI

Gold Repair Ceramic Watch

Get it on the Connect IQ Store · 2.49 €

Settings on the watch

Hold UP, pick Watch Face, select Kintsugi, press START, then Customize. The menu shows How it works, the glaze, the bottom line and the always-on brightness. The same settings are in Garmin Connect.

Broken ceramic, mended with gold

Kintsugi is the Japanese art of repairing broken ceramic with gold. This watch face is a cracked ceramic dial, and your steps are the gold. In the morning the cracks are bare. Every step pushes the gold a little further along them, from the edge of the dial inward. When you reach your step goal, every crack is gold. The percentage of your goal sits in the middle of the dial.

New cracks every day

At midnight the bowl breaks again: three new cracks with their branches, different every day, waiting for tomorrow's gold.

A classic dial

Gold baton hands and applied gold indices. Under the percentage, a bottom line of your choice: steps, watch battery, heart rate, Body Battery, calories, floors climbed, distance, intensity minutes, notifications, temperature, the next sunrise or sunset, or altitude. If your watch has no value, it shows -- rather than a made-up number.

Four glazes

Black ceramic, white porcelain, celadon and indigo.

Always-on

A separate always-on design on a black screen: only the gold you have earned so far, and the hands in grey. It shifts slightly every minute to protect the screen.

The face asks for the location permission for sunrise and sunset; the position never leaves the watch. Compatible with the Garmin AMOLED watches listed below. English only. Questions or a watch you would like to see supported: use the support e-mail on this page.
