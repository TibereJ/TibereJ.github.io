SCORE

Your Day Written as Music

Get it on the Connect IQ Store · 2.99 €

Settings on the watch

Hold UP, pick Watch Face, select Score, press START, then Customize. The menu shows How it works, the paper, the signature line, the two bottom lines, the melody, the hours and the always-on brightness. The same settings are in Garmin Connect.

A page of sheet music

The time is the time signature, large on the staff: the hours over the minutes, 10 over 08 for 10:08. Your heart rate is the tempo, exactly as a composer writes it: a quarter note = 64 beats per minute, with the Italian word for that tempo (Largo, Adagio, Andante, Moderato, Allegro, Vivace, Presto). The battery is the dynamic under the staff: ff when full, then f, mf, mp, p and pp when almost empty. The title of the piece is the day, and the opus number is the date.

The melody of your day

After the bar line, four notes for the night, the morning, the afternoon and the evening: the more you walked in that part of the day, the higher the note. A rest means no steps. The face notes your steps itself every quarter of an hour; a part of the day it did not see stays empty, never a made-up note.

Make it yours

  • Two papers: night score (light ink on black) or cream paper.
  • The signature line and two bottom lines, each your choice: steps, watch battery, heart rate, Body Battery, calories, floors climbed, distance, intensity minutes, notifications, temperature, the next sunrise or sunset, or altitude. If your watch has no value, the line shows --.
  • Hours like your watch, 24-hour or 12-hour.

Always-on

A separate always-on design on a black screen: the staff and the time signature in grey. It shifts slightly every minute to protect the screen.

The face asks for the location permission for sunrise and sunset; the position never leaves the watch. Compatible with the Garmin AMOLED watches listed below. English only. Questions or a watch you would like to see supported: use the support e-mail on this page.

Fonts: Fraunces and Jost; musical symbols: Noto Music (SIL Open Font License).
