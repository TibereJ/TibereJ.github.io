ARCADE

Retro Game Watch Face, Steps Score

Get it on the Connect IQ Store · 2.99 €

Settings on the watch

Hold UP, pick Watch Face, select Arcade, press START, then Customize. The menu shows How to play, the cabinet, the data next to the lives, the hours, the scanlines and the always-on brightness. The same settings are in Garmin Connect.

1up: your steps are the score

Your watch face is an arcade cabinet. 1UP, your score, is today's steps. HI, the high score, is your best day of the last seven: beat it. The time is huge, in chunky pixel digits with their drop shadow, and LEVEL is the day of the year.

Run to the flag

Under the time, a little runner crosses the level, eating the dots on its way. The flag at the end is your step goal: the runner moves with your steps, and when you reach the goal the screen says STAGE CLEAR.

Lives

Three hearts are the watch battery. Next to them, one data of your choice: heart rate, steps, watch battery, Body Battery, calories, floors climbed, distance, intensity minutes, notifications, temperature, the next sunrise or sunset, or altitude. If your watch has no value, it shows -- rather than a made-up number.

Make it yours

  • Four cabinets: classic violet, green screen, amber, blue.
  • Tube scanlines on or off.
  • Hours like your watch, 24-hour or 12-hour.

Always-on

A separate always-on design on a black screen: the game is on PAUSE, the time in grey. It shifts slightly every minute to protect the screen.

The pixel font and the sprites were drawn for this face. The high score is kept on the watch only. The face asks for the location permission for sunrise and sunset; the position never leaves the watch. Compatible with the Garmin AMOLED watches listed below. English only.
