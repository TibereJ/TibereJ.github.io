TABBY

Cute Cat Watch Face

Get it on the Connect IQ Store · 2.99 €

Settings on the watch

Hold UP, pick Watch Face, select Tabby, press START, then Customize. The menu shows How it works, the cat, the two data, the hours and the always-on brightness. The same settings are in Garmin Connect.

A cat that lives your day

Your cat takes up half the screen, and it changes through the day:

  • From 7:00 to 22:00, it sits and looks at you.
  • From 22:00 to 7:00, it sleeps, curled up on its cushion.
  • When you reach your step goal, it closes its eyes and waves a paw.

Three cats

Ginger, black and grey.

Two data around the cat

Steps and heart rate by default, or your choice of steps, watch battery, heart rate, Body Battery, calories, floors climbed, distance, intensity minutes, notifications, temperature, the next sunrise or sunset, altitude or the date. If your watch has no value, it shows -- rather than a made-up number. While the cat sleeps, only the first one stays on screen.

Always-on

A separate always-on design on a black screen: the time and the outline of the cat's head in grey. It shifts slightly every minute to protect the screen.

The face asks for the location permission for sunrise and sunset; the position never leaves the watch. Compatible with the Garmin AMOLED watches listed below. English only.
