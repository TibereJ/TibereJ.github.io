Watch face for Garmin watches

FLUTTER

Colorful Butterflies Watch Face

Get it on the Connect IQ Store · 2.99 €

Settings on the watch

Hold UP, pick Watch Face, select Flutter, press START, then Customize. The menu shows How it works, the colors, the bottom line, the hours and the always-on brightness. The same settings are in Garmin Connect.

Butterflies on your wrist

Bright butterflies around a big, easy-to-read time, glowing on the black of an AMOLED screen.

Your steps call the swarm

In the morning, 4 butterflies. The swarm grows as you get closer to your step goal, and all 16 are around the time when you reach it. It starts again the next day.

Three palettes

Neon, monarch and pastel.

The bottom line

Steps by default, or your choice: steps, watch battery, heart rate, Body Battery, calories, floors climbed, distance, intensity minutes, notifications, temperature, the next sunrise or sunset, altitude or the date. If your watch has no value, it shows -- rather than a made-up number.

Always-on

A separate always-on design on a black screen: the time and your data in grey. It shifts slightly every minute to protect the screen.

The face asks for the location permission for sunrise and sunset; the position never leaves the watch. Compatible with the Garmin AMOLED watches listed below. English only.
