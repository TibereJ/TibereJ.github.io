How to install a Connect IQ watch face on your Garmin

  1. Install the Connect IQ Store app on your phone and sign in with your Garmin account.
  2. Pick your watch at the top of the store.
  3. Search the face's name, open it and tap Install (or the price, for a paid face).
  4. Sync the watch with your phone. The face appears in the watch's list of watch faces: select it there.

Paid watch faces

Paid faces are bought in the Connect IQ Store. The watch needs Connect IQ 5 (System 7) or newer, and purchases are open in the United States, Canada, Mexico, the United Kingdom, the European Union, Australia and New Zealand.

Many faces here have a free version with fewer options. Try it first: if you like the look, the full version adds the themes, data choices and extras.

Next step: open the face's settings on the watch.
